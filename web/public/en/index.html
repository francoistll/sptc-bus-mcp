Bus Seychelles

Mahé buses, in one question

Ask in plain language: “next bus from Victoria to Beau Vallon”.

Seychelles time (UTC+4) by default

About this service

How it works

  1. A free AI (Cloudflare Workers AI) understands your sentence and extracts: origin, destination, time, day, route.
  2. A plain program queries the timetable database built from the official PDFs.
  3. The answer is shown as is: the AI never makes up a time.

If the free AI quota runs out, simple rules take over (“from X to Y”, “route 20”).

Sample sentences

  • next bus from Victoria to Beau Vallon
  • from Beau Vallon to Victoria around 17:30
  • timetable route 24A saturday
  • stop Glacis

Limits

  • No real-time data (delays, cancellations).
  • Direct trips only, no transfers.
  • Stop GPS positions are incomplete.

Where does the data come from?

Unofficial integration. This site is not affiliated with the Seychelles Public Transport Corporation (SPTC). When in doubt, rely on the timetables posted at stops or on the official website.

Times come from the official SPTC PDF timetables, effective 4 August 2026, published at sptc.sc/timetable:

  • Weekday timetable (PDF)
  • Weekend timetable (PDF)

SPTC publishes neither a GTFS feed nor an API: the tables in these PDFs were extracted automatically (pdfplumber), cleaned, and stored in a SQLite database. Extraction errors are possible.

Stop positions, where available, come from OpenStreetMap (© contributors, ODbL) and are often approximate.

If you are from SPTC and want this data removed or corrected, please contact the site author.
